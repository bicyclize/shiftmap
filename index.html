Draws the route the trip could have taken instead: a bike leg (on the real cycle network, which prefers cycleways and refuses bicycle=no streets), the train leg between the two stations — shown as dots — and the bike or foot leg on the far side. Turn it on by picking a scenario. Trips whose alternative could not be routed are left out rather than sketched.

Road / rail is toggled in the map legend (top right), so the same switches drive the map and every number here. Rail is read off the guideway — tram, HÉV, metro, rail.

Volumes come from routing every trip along the OSM road network — thicker = more trips. Click any road to keep only the trips routed along it; click the same road again to clear.

Click “Draw area”, then click the map to place corners; double-click (or “Finish”) to close.
